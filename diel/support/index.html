Get in touch

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email diel.support@gmail.com. You can also tap Send feedback in Diel's Settings.

Diel Pro

Manage or cancel: in Diel, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also use the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

New iPhone or reinstalled Diel? Open the Diel Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Refunds: Apple handles payments, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. To save a copy, tap Export your data in Settings. To erase everything, tap Delete all data.
