Diel Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

The short version

Diel keeps your health data on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see, sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log (fasts, water, weight, medications, cycle and meals) plus your goals and settings. It's stored in the app's private storage and shared only with Diel's own widgets and Live Activities. Nothing is synced to our servers; we don't run any.

Food search and barcodes

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts to look up nutrition info. Nothing else goes with it: no health data, no name, no account. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Exporting your data

Tap Export your data in Settings to get a PDF summary of your medications, fasts, weight and cycle. It's made on your iPhone, and you choose where it goes.

Deleting your data

Tap Delete all data in Settings to erase everything at once. Deleting the app also removes all its data from your iPhone.

Feedback and crash reports

If Diel crashes or freezes, your iPhone may give the app a short technical report (where in the code it happened, plus the app, iOS and device model). It holds none of your health data and stays on your iPhone. It only reaches us if you tap Send feedback, where it's attached to the email draft so you can see it, remove it, or not send at all.

Diel Pro subscriptions

Diel Pro is bought and managed through your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment, so we never see your name, email or card details. Diel only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active, right on your iPhone.

Children

Diel isn't made for children under 13, and since your data stays on your iPhone, we never collect personal info from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and in the App Store. Questions? Email diel.support@gmail.com.
